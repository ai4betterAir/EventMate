From group to game

A recurring sports group should almost run itself.

01👥

Create group

Creator becomes Owner/Admin and can promote other trusted members to Admin.

02🔗

Invite members

Share a group link, QR code or short code. Members join once and stay in the group.

03🔁

Schedule sessions

One-off, weekly, fortnightly or monthly. Set venue, time, courts and maximum players.

04🔔

Ask before every game

EventMate sends “Are you playing tomorrow?” at the admin-selected reminder time.

05✅

Confirm attendance

Yes, No or Maybe. Live totals update instantly and help the admin decide courts.

06🧾

Settle actual cost

After play, admin confirms actual attendance and real expenses before splitting the cost.
