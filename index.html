From group to game

A recurring sports group should almost run itself.

01👥

Create group

Creator becomes Owner/Admin and can promote other trusted members to Admin.

02🔗

Invite members

Share a group link, QR code or short code. Members join once and stay in the group.

03🔁

Schedule sessions

One-off, weekly, fortnightly or monthly. Set venue, time, courts and maximum players.

04✅

Confirm attendance

Going, Maybe or Not going. Waiting list starts automatically when capacity is full.

05🧮

Calculate share

Admin enters real session expenses after play; EventMate calculates each attendee's share.

06🧾

Verify payments

Member submits amount and receipt screenshot. Admin checks the bank and approves or rejects.
